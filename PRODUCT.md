# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Children aged 8–10 who read Ukrainian and practise beginner English vocabulary
on a parent-controlled phone, tablet, or computer. A parent supervises the
validation session and can inspect or export browser-local pilot metrics.

## Product Purpose

Test whether a short two-lane vocabulary runner is understandable and engaging
enough that children voluntarily replay it. The prototype trains recall of
previously introduced words; it does not claim to teach vocabulary by itself.
The current product decision is to make the runner interaction physically
satisfying before expanding content, platforms, or monetization.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, warm, mischievous. The game should feel quick and responsive without
ridiculing mistakes, shouting at the child, or turning every answer into a
separate quiz screen.

## Anti-references

- A static quiz laid over a moving background.
- A runner that slides between lanes while gates pass through it without impact.
- Full-screen correction cards that interrupt the sense of running.
- Repetitive jokes, spoken comic lines, harsh failure sounds, or ad-like reward
  clutter.
- A generic economy, shop, leaderboard, or streak pressure added before replay
  intent is validated.

## Design Principles

- Make the choice happen in the world: lane movement, gate response, camera, and
  pace should all acknowledge the answer.
- Keep learning feedback immediate and readable, but subordinate it to the run.
- Spend delight on physical cause and effect; one convincing interaction matters
  more than several decorative gags.
- Preserve one-thumb play, large semantic DOM targets, offline operation, and a
  quiet parent-controlled sound path.
- Treat voluntary replay as the engagement signal; treat learning efficacy and
  delayed retention as separate experiments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The child flow keeps at least 48-by-48 CSS-pixel targets, touch, swipe, and
keyboard controls, readable high-contrast text, a non-WebGL fallback, and a
complete reduced-motion mode. Mistakes never remove lives, shame the child, or
force a restart. Audio remains optional and never owns essential information.

## Lifecycle

This is a live product contract derived from the approved prototype PRD,
supervised feedback, and GitHub Issue #2. Update it only when the target user,
validation purpose, product personality, or strategic design principles change.
